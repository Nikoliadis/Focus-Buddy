# Room chat, tasks, voice and screen sharing

## Room chat
Each room has a live chat. Messages can be up to 500 characters, and when you open a room you see the last 50 messages. Only members of the room can read and write in its chat.

## Muting members
The room owner can mute and unmute members. A muted member can still read the chat but cannot send messages until they are unmuted.

## My Tasks
The My Tasks panel in a room is your personal to-do list. A task can be up to 300 characters. You can tick tasks as done, delete them, and mark a task as public or private when you add it.

## Voice and screen sharing
Inside a room you can turn your microphone on and share your screen with the other members. Audio and video go directly between the browsers (peer-to-peer WebRTC); the FocusBuddy server only helps the browsers find each other and never receives the media.

## FocusBot
FocusBot is the study assistant in the AI tab of a room. It answers questions about studying and about using FocusBuddy, and shows the help articles it used for its answer.
