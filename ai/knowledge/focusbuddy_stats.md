# Statistics, goals and leaderboard

## The Stats page
The Stats page shows your focused time today, in the last 7 days and in total, your best single day, and how many sessions you completed. A chart shows focused minutes per day for the last 7 days, and a table shows your focus time per room and your 10 most recent sessions.

## Streaks
Your current streak is the number of consecutive days, up to today or yesterday, on which you focused at least once. Your best streak is the longest such run you have ever had.

## Daily goal
You can set a daily focus goal in minutes on your Profile page. The Stats page then shows a progress bar with how much of today's goal you have reached. Setting the goal to 0 removes it.

## Exporting your data
Use Export CSV on the Stats page to download all your focus sessions with their date, room and duration in seconds and minutes.

## Leaderboard
The leaderboard shows the top 10 users by total focus time, with each user's focus time this week next to their all-time total.
