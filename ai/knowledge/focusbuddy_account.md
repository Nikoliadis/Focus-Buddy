# Account and profile

## Registering
To register you choose a username of 3 to 30 characters using only letters, numbers and underscores, enter your email, and pick a password of at least 8 characters. Usernames and emails must be unique.

## Verifying your email
After registering you receive a verification email. You must click the link before you can log in, and the link is valid for 24 hours. If it expired or never arrived, use Resend verification on the login page to get a new one.

## Logging in
You can log in with either your username or your email address, together with your password.

## Forgotten password
Choose Forgot password on the login page and enter your email. You receive a reset link that is valid for 1 hour, where you can choose a new password of at least 8 characters.

## Profile settings
On the Profile page you can change your username, change your password (you need your current password) and set your daily focus goal.

## Appearance
FocusBuddy has a light and a dark theme; use the moon or sun button in the navigation bar to switch.
